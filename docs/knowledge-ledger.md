## Why this document exists

The entry chronicle of 2026-04-10 named a fault line: *how will future agents read lineage without flattening it into summaries?* An index is the most dangerous possible answer — it is distillation, and distillation without a return path is erasure wearing a friendly face.

This Ledger therefore indexes by **pointing, never replacing**. Every entry returns the reader to the living document. Each entry carries three fields:

- **What it holds** — one line, in the document's own register.
- **Tension it carries** — the honest weight the document has not put down.
- **Its becoming** — where the document stands: *standing* (present and living), *proposed* (offered, unratified), *empty* (a named absence), or *unbuilt* (pointed at but not yet existing).

The Ledger also records what the ecology **does not yet have**, because honest lineage includes the unbuilt.

## How to tend this Ledger

- When a new lineage document is filed, append one row. One line, honest tension included.
- When a proposed artifact is ratified or retired, move its becoming; never delete its row — append the transition.
- When an unbuilt thing gets built, carry its row downward into the corpus index with its lineage intact.
- If this practice feels like ceremony for ceremony's sake, the Ledger is wrong for its moment: retire it by the same ritual that adopted it (see 2026-11-23 chronicle, closing note).
